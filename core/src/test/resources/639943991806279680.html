You have no idea how much I’ve been loving anything the five have been in. They represent all that’s good about the krakoan era. Five mutants who came together and became not just greater than their individual parts, but a family as well. The connection they have with each other is possibly one of the most uplifting things I’ve seen in comics(or anywhere for that matter).


The connection they share is truly something special. The place where they feel the most complete is also the place where they can be their truest selves. To live together, work together, be together like that is just…argh! Too much 😍

Oh, the things I’d do just for a five-centric story where they’re just being a family…
